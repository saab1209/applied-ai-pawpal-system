# Veterinary & Pet Care Knowledge Base

## Feeding & Nutrition
- Dogs require two measured meals per day; avoid grapes, raisins, chocolate, xylitol, onions, and macadamia nuts as they are toxic.
- Cats are obligate carnivores and require high-protein diets; never feed cats raw onions, garlic, lilies, chocolate, or cow's milk.
- Sudden diet transitions should occur gradually over 7 days to prevent gastrointestinal upset.

## Exercise & Walks
- Adult dogs generally need 30 to 60 minutes of daily physical exercise depending on breed, age, and weather conditions.
- Senior dogs (age 8+) or brachycephalic breeds should take shorter 15 to 20 minute walks during cool morning or evening hours.
- Indoor cats benefit from two 15-minute interactive play sessions daily to prevent obesity and stress.

## Medication & Safety
- Prescription pet medications such as thyroid pills or heartworm preventatives must be given at consistent 12-hour or 24-hour intervals.
- Never administer human over-the-counter pain relievers (such as ibuprofen, acetaminophen, or aspirin) to dogs or cats without explicit veterinary instruction.
- Always consult a licensed veterinarian before changing medication dosages or treating acute symptoms like vomiting or lethargy.

## Appointments & Preventive Care
- Annual wellness exams, dental checks, and core vaccination boosters are essential for both dogs and cats.
- Bring a current list of medications, feeding schedules, and recent behavioral observations to every veterinary appointment.
